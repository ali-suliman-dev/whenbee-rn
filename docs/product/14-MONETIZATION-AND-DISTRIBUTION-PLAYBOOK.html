Do

  • Chase medical-device or reimbursement ground.

01

Rules of the game

Two sets. Product invariants that nothing here may touch, and the four constraints that decide which tactics are even available.

Locked. This doc does not argue with these.

  • Calibration free, all categories, full depth. Pro sells the payoff layer.
  • $4.99/mo, $34.99/yr hero, $89 lifetime, $49 founder. Read from RevenueCat.
  • No guilt, no streaks, amber never red, honey only climbs.
  • Core loop: no network call, no LLM.
  • No paywall at install, in onboarding, in the first session, during a timer, after a log, or on a calibration insight.
ConstraintKillsForces, usefully
Solo, small budgetAlways-on paid UA, agencies, PR retainers, big creator dealsChannels that keep working while you sleep: store search, AI answers, evergreen video, permanent listings
No build-in-publicMRR screenshots, day-N threads, Indie Hackers as a channelMarketing to users instead of to founders. Better anyway.
No face on cameraTalking-head content, day-in-the-life, podcast videoScreen-first video, which is what sells a utility. The number changing on screen is the product.
AI content allowedNothing, given disclosure (see A2)Volume. One person can ship 20 to 40 variants a week.

No face does not mean no human. Voice-only works everywhere: podcast guesting is audio, Reddit is text, a hand holding a phone is not a face. Anonymous accounts are normal in the ADHD space, where plenty of people keep a diagnosis off their real name.

S1

Store surfaces

freehigh effort oncehighest payoffblocks S3, S7, S8

Four things changed in Apple's store since mid-2025 and most apps have not adjusted. That gap is the opening.

  1. Treat screenshot captions as metadataApple began OCR-indexing caption text in June 2025. Apple has not confirmed it; ASO teams measured apps ranking for terms that existed only in overlays. Write captions in words people search. First screenshot has one job, earning the tap in search results, so show the outcome: the honest number beside the guess. No stuffing, it converts badly and conversion is itself a ranking signal.
  2. Build custom product pages, one per intentSince July 2025 a CPP can carry assigned keywords and surface in organic search instead of the default listing. Limit went to 70 in October 2025 and only about a third of apps use CPPs at all. Someone searching "always running late" needs a different first screen than someone searching "task timer". Build 4 to 6, not 70.
  3. Run the Honest Week as an in-app eventEvents are indexed on name and short description, appear on Today, in search, and on your page, and Apple picks a best weekly event. Most utility apps skip events because they assume events mean game tournaments. You already built a cadenced weekly ritual.
  4. Submit a featuring nominationApp Store Connect has the form now, so it is a queue rather than a lottery. Three weeks minimum, up to three months for wide consideration. Editors score seven things: UX, UI, innovation, uniqueness, accessibility, localisation, product page. Needs Account Holder, Admin, App Manager or Marketing role.
  5. Fix the iPad layout, and count it as growth workIt is on the launch blocker list already. It is also a featuring criterion, and featured apps skew heavily to iPhone-plus-iPad availability.
  6. Write Play's long description for indexingPlay indexes the whole long description, has native listing experiments and custom store listings per audience, and only accepts nominations for new apps. Its discovery leans algorithmic where Apple leans editorial.

Intents worth their own page

time blindnessadhd time managementtask timer how long will this taketime estimate tracker always running latefocus timer no streaks

The pitch shape editors reward

Editors need a story they can write and one signature thing visible in thirty seconds. Yours is not the timer. It is that the app learns how wrong you are about time and quietly corrects your plans, on device, with no AI and no shaming. Nominate around a real hook: the Android live-timer presence work, the no-guilt accessibility angle, or a mental health awareness moment, which Apple reliably curates.

Retention is now a ranking input in both stores, alongside downloads. So the D7 gate and store ranking are one project, and buying installs early actively damages the signal that earns free ones.

S2

AI answer visibility

freewriting and email onlycompoundsno camera needed

People ask a chatbot "what app helps with time blindness" instead of searching a store. Models recommend apps that recur across roundups with consistent positioning. A 4.8-star app with 17,000 ratings can be invisible if it never appears in a list.

  1. Baseline it, free, in an hourTen phrasings of the user's question across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity. Save the answers and every source each model cites. That citation list is your target list.
  2. Get into the roundups models quoteUsually ADHD and productivity listicles plus comparison sites. Pitch a specific angle, not a press release: the one calibration app that runs on device with no AI, no streaks, and a real multiplier. Offer a free Pro code and answer the unflattering questions straight.
  3. Claim the comparison surfacesAlternativeTo and similar rank forever for "alternatives to X" and are cited heavily. List Whenbee, then list it as an alternative to the obvious incumbents. Best hour on the whole list.
  4. Say the identical sentence everywhereModels reward consistency. Store, site, every directory, every roundup, same line: Whenbee learns how wrong your time estimates are and gives you the honest number, on your device.
  5. Publish one page per questionOn whenbee.app: what is time blindness, why do I underestimate how long things take, what is a time-estimation multiplier. Plain question-and-answer shape. Same work as SEO, pays twice now.
  6. Re-measure monthlySame ten prompts, same models. Share of answers naming Whenbee is the metric. AppTweak launched ChatGPT app-recommendation tracking in April 2026 if you ever want it instrumented properly.

Useful side effect: Reddit is reportedly the most-cited domain across major models, so the community work in S4 feeds this system too. A good comment about estimation math today can be quoted inside a chatbot answer next year.

S3

Faceless video engine

$0–60/modaily-ish, batchedhighest upside, highest waste risk

Read this before making anything

An app-marketing operator posted in July 2026: 1.5 billion views, 2,000+ videos, 14% engagement, and the app barely converted. His line is the one to keep: views without product attribution are just building someone else's audience. Every asset needs a path to install that you can see.

What converts in 2026

Polished testimonial creative burned out. Ring-light reviews and scripted walkthroughs get scrolled. AppsFlyer's 2025 analysis found tutorials and reviews produce about 45% higher IPM and 17% better day-seven retention than testimonials. Method-first content earns slightly fewer clicks and much better users, which is the exact trade you want with a D7 gate.

FormatFaceless buildPlatformWhenbee line
Expert commentaryVoice over screen recording, podcast-clip framing, burned captionsYouTube Shorts"Time blindness is not a discipline problem, it is a measurement problem. Here is what 30 logged tasks looked like."
Scenario skitText-on-screen scenario, no actorTikTok, Reels"Me: this takes 20 minutes. The clock: 47. Every time."
Pattern interruptStarts as something else, turns into the number revealTikTokTimer runs past the guess, then the honest number appears and matches reality.
Street interviewNeeds faces. Skip.n/an/a

TikTok and Reels want the hook inside three seconds and short skits. Shorts rewards longer explanation and shows the highest engagement of the three. One concept, three native cuts, never one video posted three times.

The pipeline

  1. Record once, properlyFifteen to twenty clean captures: the guess, the timer, the honest number appearing, the honeycomb ripening, the day rebuilding itself. That is a year of raw material. Do not keep re-recording.
  2. Write thirty hooksOne line each, confessional, lowercase, doc-06 voice. Bank below.
  3. Assemble cheaplyScreen capture plus AI voice plus burned captions. Capture-to-narrated-cut tooling runs under half an hour per video. Stay off avatar platforms and the stack costs $0 to $60 a month.
  4. Attribute everythingOne link per format per platform. Bio link points at a page on whenbee.app, not straight at the store, so you see the click before the store swallows it.
  5. Review weekly on hold rate, not viewsThree-second hold and saves. Kill weak hooks, then make five variants of anything that holds.

On AI avatars, a recommendation

Synthetic-creator platforms run about $39/mo at entry and past $100 for better avatars, against roughly $200 per video for a human creator. They are built for e-commerce and the avatars still read uncanny on close watch. An uncanny person telling an ADHD audience they were never broken will land badly. Use AI for voice, captions, scripting, editing, translation. Let the screen be the star. If you test an avatar anyway: disclose it, keep it in paid only, and expect it to lose to a plain screen recording.

Hook bank, starter ten

  • i said 20 minutes. it was 47. it is always 47.
  • i am not bad at time. i am wrong about it by the same amount every time.
  • my phone tells me the real number before i promise anyone anything.
  • the app watched me for two weeks and worked out i run 1.8x over. rude. correct.
  • nobody needs to tell me to try harder. i needed one honest multiplier.
  • this is what "i'll be there in 10 minutes" actually means, measured.
  • i stopped estimating. i just look at what it says now.
  • your morning collapses because of one task, and you can find out which.
  • no streaks. nothing dies if you skip a day. that was the design brief.
  • it runs on your phone. nothing leaves it. no ai in the part that matters.
S4

Communities and creators

$0–500slow, high trustfeeds S2

Someone else already spent years gathering people who are late and tired of being told to try harder. Rent that, do not rebuild it.

Reddit and the rest, six rules

The full community map, per-sub rules and exact post copy already live in market-research-2026/LAUNCH-AND-POSTING-PLAN.md. Follow it rather than rewriting it. The part people skip:

  1. Nine to oneNine honest posts or comments for every one that mentions the app. In ADHD subs that is survival, not etiquette.
  2. Age and karma firstMost good subs auto-remove new accounts.
  3. Lead with your own struggleThe app is a footnote or absent.
  4. Say "I built this"This audience has excellent radar and forgives honesty faster than anything else.
  5. No link in a launch post bodyComment, or wait to be asked.
  6. Block the hour after postingEarly replies decide whether a thread lives.

One rule, no exceptions

A unique code or link per partner, every time. Skip it and three months later you cannot tell which channel worked, and you will guess wrong.

S5

Paywall and pricing

free to changeconfig, not buildbiggest revenue lever

2026 data says hard paywalls win on revenue by a lot. You are not going to use one, and that is the right call. So take the hit knowingly and claw it back with six levers that never touch an invariant.

RevenueCat 2026. Hard paywall means unusable until you subscribe.
MetricHard paywallFreemium
D35 trial to paid10.7%2.1%
Revenue per install, D60$3.09$0.38

Why keep the free tier anyway: free calibration is not a pricing choice, it is the thesis. The app proves it knows you before it asks for anything. Wall it and the D7 gate almost certainly fails on an unproven audience. This audience is also subscription-burned, so a wall reads as a trap, and the no-trap promise is load-bearing for trust.

  1. Move the value earlier, not the paywallProductivity is the category where 71.9% of eventual payers pay on day zero, and your paywall is deliberately absent then. Resolution: make the "make my whole day honest" moment and the capacity check reachable and visible in session one or two, locked but legible. Locked with a clear price of admission converts. Hidden does not exist.
  2. Experiment on the paywall, not the pricesPrices stay locked and read from RevenueCat. Headline, order of proof, whether lifetime sits beside yearly or under it, one screen or two: all testable server-side with no release. You already ship two feature variants behind the developer toggle, so the harness exists.
  3. Treat intro and win-back offers as plumbingA win-back for churned subscribers, an offer for people who viewed the paywall three times and never bought, a longer trial for heavy loggers who do not convert.
  4. Localise price points, not only stringsPer-storefront pricing through RevenueCat. Sweden is already on the i18n plan. Highest margin work here because it is config.
  5. Keep lifetime capped at 25% of mix$89 lifetime is your only real lump of early cash and this audience prefers it. Past the cap you are selling next year's income for this month's.
  6. Annual stays the defaultAnnual churns far less. Monthly stays visible but small so it anchors instead of competing.

Scale check

Median revenue per install is $0.23 by day 14 and $0.34 by day 60. Freemium at those numbers needs volume. Whenbee's bet is not volume, it is a high-intent niche with above-median willingness to pay. If your RPI lands near the median, fix the payoff bundle or the paywall, not the install count.

S6

Off-store revenue

no store cutemails and a web pageafter D7 clears

The Pro bundle already contains the artifact a professional needs: a PDF of a person's real time patterns. Coaches and therapists currently try to extract that from a client's memory, which is the one place it does not exist.

  1. Version one: a page and a pile of codesA coaches page on whenbee.app. "Your client cannot tell you how long their morning takes. This can." Free Pro codes to any coach who asks, tracked per recipient. You are buying distribution, not selling software.
  2. Version two, only if version one bitesA practitioner tier: client codes in bundles at a per-seat price, billed through Stripe on the web. No store cut, and the coach becomes your salesperson.
  3. Promo codes as your only attribution currencyApp Store and Play promo codes cost nothing and are how you measure organic mentions at all.

Vendor research puts coaching bundles at three to five times the monthly revenue of a standard premium subscription. Treat the multiple as directional, it comes from a paid market report. The mechanism holds regardless: the payer is not the person with time blindness, and they are buying back their own session hours.

Worth trying

  • Coach and clinician door
  • Gift codes as marketing currency
  • Localised pricing
  • Lifetime, inside the cap

Ruled out, with reasons

  • Medical device or reimbursement. Needs clinical validation and a compliance surface one person cannot staff.
  • Lifetime-deal marketplaces. Sells your best future revenue at a deep discount to buyers with no loyalty.
  • Ads in the app. Wrong product, and rounding-error money at your scale.
  • A spendable Reclaim economy. Doc 03 already killed it, correctly.
  • Consulting to fund the app. Funds this month, costs you the app.

Paid ads

$300+/molow effortlocked until payback is known

Apple Ads is the one paid channel that fits a solo operator: no creative production, exact attribution, and stable keywords once found. Everything else waits for creative that already worked organically.

Apple Ads benchmarks, 2026. Category and country swing these hard.
MetricAverageExcellent
Tap-through rate9%15%+
Conversion rate64%75%+
Cost per tap$1.34–1.5150% under category
Cost per install$2.00–3.50under $1.00
  1. Search results placement onlyToday tab and Search tab convert badly and product-page slots sit below the fold. One practitioner reports Search tab CPAs three to five times higher than Search results for the same app.
  2. Brand defence first, at any budgetIf you do not bid on "whenbee", competitors will. Apps have lost half their impression share on their own name. Cheapest, highest-return campaign you will run. Insurance, basically.
  3. Then a handful of exact-match intent keywordsSmall daily cap. Relevance and tap-through lower your cost over time, so a matched custom product page makes the ads cheaper. S1 pays off again here.
  4. Competitor names, tested and mostly discardedAllowed by Apple. Works when a brand name is shorthand for a category, fails when the person wants that specific app. Expect half to fail.
  5. Write the kill rule before you spendAny keyword with no paying user at 3x target cost gets paused. You will not want to follow this later, which is why it goes in writing now.

04

Metrics

Watch

  • D1, D7, D30. Targets: above 25%, toward 25%, above 8%.
  • Revenue per install at D14 and D60.
  • Store conversion, impressions to install, per custom product page.
  • Three-second hold rate and saves per video.
  • Installs per creator code or partner link.
  • Share of AI answers naming Whenbee, ten fixed prompts.
  • Paywall view to purchase, split by trigger.
  • Resurrection rate: lapsed seven days, then returned.

Ignore

  • Video views. See S3.
  • Follower counts anywhere.
  • Time in app and session length. Doc 03 rules these out. The goal is being on time in real life.
  • Product Hunt rank.
  • Downloads quoted without a retention number beside them.
  • Anything a founder-audience channel reports back.

PostHog already tracks install, first log, aha, cell capped, paywall view, purchase. Add campaign source at install and creator code at redemption, or none of the above is measurable.
