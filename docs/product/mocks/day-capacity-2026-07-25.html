Why it fails
  • Denominator never moves — 840 min regardless of clock.
  • Completed tasks leave the numerator → gets more optimistic as the day burns.
  • Ignores dayEndMin, which the Start-By planner already uses.
  • ✕ is session-permanent. A chip that only ever reassures gets dismissed once, forever.

  • wakingWindowMin → max(0, dayEndMin − nowMin). One line in useDayCapacity.
