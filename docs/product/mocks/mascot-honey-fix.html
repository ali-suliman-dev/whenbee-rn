And the three structural moves

  • 1Staple the unlock to the number. Header ring, honeycomb strip, reward payoff card. The ladder already exists in engine/companion.ts:28-33 — it's just on a different screen from the number it gates.
  • 2Demote the bee from hero to mark on the Whenbee tab, rename the tab to Progress, and lead with Discoveries. The bee keeps every momentary appearance: icon, onboarding, archetype crest, reward burst, empty states.
  • 3Give the bee one rule. It appears only when something honest just happened — a log, a tier-up, a discovery, a drift re-check. Never idle, never scolding, never needing upkeep. Rarity is what makes a mascot land.
