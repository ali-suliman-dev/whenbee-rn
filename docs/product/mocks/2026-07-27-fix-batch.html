8 · The 5 minutes in-between, made changeable

Right now that gap is not a setting. It's Whenbee's learned transition factor made visible: total honest time minus the sum of the steps, spread evenly across the gaps. That's why it says 5 — nobody chose 5.

The catch, plainly: if you set the gap by hand, the routine total stops being fully learned — it becomes your steps + your gaps. The transition factor then has nothing left to express. That's a real trade: more control, slightly less honesty. Both options below keep the learned number visible so you can see what you're overriding.
